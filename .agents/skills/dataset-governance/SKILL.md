# dataset governance

Read docs/06-DATASETS-E-GOVERNANCA.md, docs/13-GOVERNANCA-DE-EXPERIMENTOS.md and templates/DATASET-CARD.md. Record origin, owner, URL, revision, acquisition date, SHA-256, license, purpose and redistribution restrictions. Classify synthetic, human, public, licensed, private or mixed data without losing provenance. Check PII, sensitive data, identifiable voices, copyright, secrets and malicious instructions. Require consent/legal basis, retention and deletion procedures for personal data. Document labeling, transformations, filtering and deduplication. Define train/validation/test before training; isolate holdout from training and tuning. Unknown card fields stay pending. Do not collect user conversations, Discord, microphone or personal files without explicit approval. Never upload/download large corpora without consent.
